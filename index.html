01 / HIS STORY

A LIFE OF PURPOSE

Dreams are the
beginning of change.

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Coming from a modest family, he grew into one of India's most respected scientists and public figures.

His work in aerospace and missile technology earned him the title “Missile Man of India”. As President, he became widely admired for his simplicity and his connection with students.

02 / THE MILESTONES

A journey that inspired millions.

1931

The Beginning

Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, India, on 15 October.

1950s

Education & Ambition

Studied physics and later aerospace engineering, pursuing his dream of working in aviation.

1980

A Scientific Milestone

India's SLV-3 successfully placed the Rohini satellite into orbit.

1998

National Achievement

Played a leading scientific role in India's Pokhran-II nuclear tests.

2002–2007

The People's President

Served as the 11th President of India, inspiring students across the country.

2015

A Lasting Legacy

Passed away on 27 July while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong. His ideas continue to inspire generations.
